## Why this repository exists

CR is the single source of truth for the journey: find real business problems, test whether people will pay, select a narrow first offer from a broad service capability catalogue, build and deploy with AI-assisted engineering, acquire customers responsibly, collect revenue, reinvest carefully, and record what actually happened. CR is an AI-native technical-solutions business—not limited to chatbots or one technology. The broad portfolio is an intended capability map, not proof of demand or a commitment to launch every offer.

The goal is not to make a convincing startup story. The goal is to maintain a durable, auditable record of evidence and decisions—including negative findings and reasons to stop.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. **Evidence over enthusiasm.** Label facts, vendor claims, assumptions, and unknowns separately.
2. **Customer problem before code.** Do not treat a large market or an impressive demo as proof of demand.
3. **Do not fabricate validation.** A compliment is not a sale; a lead is not a customer; a trial is not collected revenue.
4. **Human review for consequential actions.** AI-generated contract interpretations, deadlines, financial calculations and customer messages require appropriate checks.
5. **Use only legitimate business contact routes.** Respect privacy, consent, provider terms and applicable telecom/commercial-communication requirements. No scraped private personal numbers or unsolicited bulk AI calling.
6. **Keep costs low.** Free/local/lightweight tools first; buy services only when justified.
7. **Protect customer information.** Use redacted samples during validation; never commit customer documents, credentials, API keys or personal data.
8. **Record failed experiments.** A reasoned stop or pivot is valuable evidence.
9. **Keep studies sustainable.** The business must fit alongside college and health.
10. **Separate revenue from profit.** ₹1 crore is an aggressive stretch target for cumulative revenue collected by 31 December 2027; profit, taxes, expenses, refunds, and cash available are tracked separately.

## Status vocabulary

- **Unresearched:** no meaningful investigation yet.
- **Desk researched:** secondary sources examined; no buyer validation implied.
- **Interviewed:** direct discussion recorded, with permission and identifying details minimised.
- **Problem signal:** repeated evidence of pain, but no confirmed commercial commitment.
- **Pilot committed:** a buyer has explicitly agreed to a scoped pilot; distinguish verbal interest from signed/paid commitment.
- **Paid:** payment has actually cleared and is recorded.
- **Retained:** customer continues to use/pay; define the measure for the product.
- **Invalidated:** evidence contradicts the hypothesis; explain the decision and next move.
