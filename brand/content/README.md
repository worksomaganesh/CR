# Founder Brand Content System

**Status:** Initial editorial framework; no publishing cadence is committed  
**Last reviewed:** 17 November 2026

## Content pillars

### 1. Building in public
Real progress on CR and technical projects: why a hypothesis was challenged, what changed after research, implementation decisions, debugging lessons and incomplete work.

### 2. Practical AI engineering
Useful concepts explained through projects: agents, APIs, retrieval, evaluation, security, integrations, testing, cost and reliability.

### 3. Business problems and technology decisions
How to map a workflow, compare existing alternatives, identify gaps and distinguish vendor claims, desk research and buyer evidence.

### 4. Learning and founder reflections
Lessons from studying AIML while building projects: concepts learned through execution, scope decisions and reflections grounded in genuine experience.

Suggested starting mix: 35% building in public, 30% practical AI engineering, 20% business/technology decisions and 15% learning reflections. Treat this as a hypothesis and adjust using audience quality and meaningful conversations.

## Calendar and cadence

Start with one substantial, evidence-backed post per week. A second short update is optional when a genuine milestone exists. Do not manufacture content to meet a quota.

| Week of | Topic / ID | Pillar | Audience | Evidence | Draft due | Review | Publish date | Platform | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| YYYY-MM-DD | | | | | | | | | Planned |

Status values: Planned, Researching, Drafting, Review, Approved, Scheduled, Published or Rejected.

Weekly questions:
1. What genuinely happened that could help the intended reader?
2. What public evidence supports it?
3. What is the one useful takeaway?
4. Does it expose private information?
5. Can it be adapted for another platform without becoming repetitive?

## Draft and publication workflow

- Store unpublished drafts in [drafts/](drafts/README.md).
- Store verified live posts in [published/](published/README.md).
- A scheduled post is not published.
- Record the exact live URL and date after publication.
- Review meaningful replies, profile visits, project visits and qualified inquiries monthly.
