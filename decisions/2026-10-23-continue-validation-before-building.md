# Decision: Continue validation before building

**Date:** 2026-10-23  
**Status:** Active; review after customer discovery  
**Decision:** Do not build the full GeM Seller OrderGuard product yet.

## Context
Desk research shows a substantial GeM MSME ecosystem and official reports of seller incidents. However, this does not establish the target user's recurring pain, the existence of a defensible gap, or willingness to pay.

## Options considered
1. Build immediately.
2. Continue desk research and interview prospective users.
3. Pivot immediately to invoice automation or AI calling.

## Rationale
Option 2 preserves scarce time and capital while reducing the risk of building a product that existing portal features, spreadsheets, consultants or accounting tools already cover. Generic invoice/receivables automation appears to have direct competition in the first scan.

## Evidence that would change this decision
- Multiple independent target users report the same costly recurring problem.
- Existing alternatives leave a specific, important task unresolved.
- A user agrees to test a realistic workflow and at least one credible buyer considers a paid pilot.
- Or, contrary evidence shows the problem is already solved or not a priority; then pivot or stop.

## Risks
The market may be too difficult to reach; the problem may not justify separate software; contract interpretation mistakes may create liability; and a student founder may lack time or budget for integrations.

## Next review
After initial customer-discovery evidence is recorded.
