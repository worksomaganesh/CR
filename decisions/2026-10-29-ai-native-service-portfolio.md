# Decision — Document CR's AI-native service portfolio

**Date:** 29 October 2026  
**Status:** Accepted as strategic direction; individual offers and first commercial focus remain unvalidated.

## Context

The founder clarified that CR should not be limited to one AI product, chatbot or RAG architecture. The intended business is an AI-native technical-solutions company that uses suitable AI tools and other technologies to solve real business problems, deploy solutions, sell measurable outcomes and adopt future technologies when they provide demonstrable value.

Requested capability areas include AI marketing/content, knowledge infrastructure, sales infrastructure, customer operations, websites/forms/integrations, reporting/analytics including Power BI, and custom AI/software/data solutions.

## Decision

1. Maintain a broad, explicit service/capability catalogue in [docs/service-catalogue.md](../docs/service-catalogue.md).
2. Position CR around business outcomes rather than technology names.
3. Keep the active commercial focus narrow: one or two offers at a time, chosen using buyer evidence, competitive gaps, feasibility, delivery cost and willingness to pay.
4. Prefer a service-assisted paid pilot before productising; pursue subscriptions only after repeated value and retention are evidenced.
5. Choose Excel/Sheets, Power BI, managed analytics or another approach based on actual requirements, not perceived prestige. Price data quality, modelling, validation, deployment, licensing and support work explicitly.
6. Use AI as an execution accelerator, but independently verify important outputs and keep the founder accountable for customer commitments, security, compliance, production decisions and delivery.

## Alternatives considered

- **Commit to one product immediately:** rejected for now because existing candidate hypotheses are not validated and current research has identified competitive overlap.
- **Sell every service at once:** rejected because it would scatter marketing, learning, delivery and support capacity.
- **Position as only a RAG/chatbot provider:** rejected because it artificially limits the business and focuses on implementation method rather than customer outcome.
- **Build a broad SaaS platform first:** rejected in favour of narrow paid validation and service-first learning.

## Consequences

- The catalogue is a map of intended capabilities, not proof of demand, fixed pricing, or a promise that every offer will launch.
- Each offer needs a defined buyer, current workflow, problem evidence, alternatives, measurable outcome, scope, acceptance criteria, cost model, data/security review and support plan.
- No current product hypothesis is automatically selected by this decision.
- New technologies may be adopted when a small benchmark or customer project shows a meaningful improvement in cost, quality, speed, reliability or capability.

## Review / reversal criteria

Review the direction if customer discovery shows a more valuable narrow focus, delivery economics are unsustainable, a service creates unacceptable risk, or the founder's time/resources cannot support the proposed offer. Preserve the dated record and add a new decision rather than rewriting this history.
