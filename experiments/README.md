# Experiments

Every experiment should answer one decision-relevant question and have a pre-defined pass/fail interpretation.

For each test record:
- Hypothesis
- Target user/segment
- Method and sample
- Start/end dates
- Cost and time spent
- Raw observations (minimised/anonymised)
- Results and limitations
- What changed in our beliefs
- Decision: continue, modify, pivot, or stop
- Next experiment

Use [the experiment template](../templates/experiment-template.md). Do not change success criteria after seeing results without documenting why.
