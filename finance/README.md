# Finance, service economics and ₹1 crore target

CR's intended offers are documented in the [service catalogue](../docs/service-catalogue.md). Revenue may eventually come from scoped paid pilots and implementations, monthly marketing/content retainers, managed AI workflows, analytics/reporting projects, maintenance and product subscriptions after repeat demand is proven. These are candidate models, not forecast revenue.

## Target definition
Stretch target: ₹1 crore cumulative revenue actually collected by 31 December 2027. It is not a forecast or a promise. Revenue is not profit or personal take-home.

## Track monthly
- Opening cash
- Invoices issued
- Cash collected
- Refunds/credits
- Payment fees and taxes
- Software/API/hosting/telephony costs
- Contractor and other operating costs
- Net operating cash flow
- Cash reserved for taxes/statutory obligations
- Reinvestment budget
- Closing cash
- Active paying customers, churn and renewals

## Service pricing and unit economics

For each quote, estimate setup and delivery time, customer onboarding, testing, data cleaning, model/API usage, hosting/storage, connectors, messaging/voice charges, software licences, support, monitoring, payment fees, taxes/statutory liabilities and contingency. State which third-party subscriptions the customer owns and which costs CR resells or passes through. Define usage caps and recurring support scope; do not promise unlimited usage without a cost model.

For analytics, price the work rather than the extension: Excel/Sheets reporting, Power BI data modeling/measures, publishing/sharing, data refresh and managed monthly analysis can have different scopes and costs. A `.pbix` handover alone does not include hosting, licences, refresh or support unless stated.

## Rules
- Record only payments actually received as collected revenue.
- Distinguish verbal interest, proposals, signed agreements, invoices and cleared payments.
- Do not take on debt or spend heavily to make revenue projections look achievable.
- Reinvest only after accounting for obligations and operating runway.
- Seek qualified accounting/legal guidance when required.
