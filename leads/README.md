# Lead research

This folder is for building a verified list of businesses that plausibly fit the ICP—not for collecting as many names as possible.

## Rules
- Start with 20 well-matched business prospects, not hundreds of unverified AI-generated names.
- Each lead needs an official business source URL and evidence of relevance.
- Record only published business contact channels; do not collect private personal numbers or sensitive personal details.
- Treat inferred pain as a hypothesis. A company being a GeM seller does not prove it has the proposed problem.
- Verify every AI-generated company, website, category and contact detail manually.
- Do not contact anyone from a generated list without human review.
- Track source date and confidence.
- Remove stale, duplicate or irrelevant leads.
