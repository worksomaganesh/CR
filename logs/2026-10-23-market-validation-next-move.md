## Key findings
1. For FY 2025–26 / AY 2026–27, the old 3CA/3CB/3CD framework applies. For Tax Year 2026–27, Form No. 26 applies; official rules locate the MSME disclosures in Part D, Clause 33.
2. CalcGuru advertises its FY 2025–26 workbook as free and claims substantial coverage, weakening the old Form 3CD calculator idea.
3. Tally supports MSME details, effective dates/history, due-date and overdue tracking, MSME reports and Form 1 annexure exports.
4. Figment advertises bulk vendor verification and current Form 26 / Section 37(2)(g) support, so Udyam lookup or form mapping alone is not a differentiated wedge.
5. No strong, confirmed market gap has been established. The remaining hypothesis is a CA-facing exception/reconciliation / working-paper QA workflow for data across existing accounting tools and the new reporting format. It must be disproved in discovery.

## Problems / limitations
- Official pages and vendor descriptions establish published form rules and advertised capabilities, not how many CAs have unmet needs.
- Vendor product pages are not independent efficacy or adoption evidence.
- The official form PDF was readable through extracted text; web screenshot attempts returned cache errors, so this review relies on the official PDF text view and Income Tax Department FAQ.
- Prospect websites were checked for public business enquiry routes and broad service relevance only. Their exact tax-audit volume, software stacks and pain are unknown.
- Some listed firms may be poor-fit or may already have excellent tooling. These are discovery candidates, not confirmed leads/customers.
