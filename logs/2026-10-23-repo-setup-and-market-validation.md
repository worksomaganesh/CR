## Lessons
- A large market does not establish a gap.
- An incident count does not establish software-addressable pain.
- Competitor pages show advertised features, not independent product performance.
- Lead generation is not sales conversion; customer discovery must precede product commitment.
- The repository must preserve assumptions and negative findings, not just progress stories.

## Blockers / limitations

- Current feature/pricing claims must be rechecked before any purchase or product decision.
- Lead generation has not been completed; no prospect should be represented as contacted.
