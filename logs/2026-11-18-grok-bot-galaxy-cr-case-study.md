## Decisions and boundaries
- Adopt the operating principles, not the 72-hour deadline.
- Use a small number of clear roles; roles are not separate always-on agents yet.
- Keep CR GitHub as canonical public/sanitized knowledge and keep confidential customer information elsewhere.
- Require founder approval for external communications, publishing, commercial commitments, merges/releases, production actions and money movement.
- No new paid tools or infrastructure are assumed.
- No autonomous agents were connected/launched; no customer outreach sent; no product selected or validated.
