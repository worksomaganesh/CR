# Research

This folder records market landscapes, customer segments, competitor comparisons, source registers, product hypotheses and validation methods.

## Rules
- Every material claim should have a source and a date.
- Label evidence as official/independent, vendor-published, customer-reported, or founder assumption.
- Vendor marketing is evidence of an advertised offer, not proof of results.
- Keep original dated reports. Add a new version when evidence changes; do not silently rewrite history.
- Record what would invalidate each hypothesis.
- Do not infer willingness to pay from market size or a prospect list.
