## Discovery questions that matter most
1. Does Tally/Excel already produce the exact outputs the firm needs?
2. Is the gap about missing inputs, cross-file reconciliation, the new Form No. 26 mapping, evidence provenance, or merely unfamiliarity with the updated forms?
3. What does the firm already pay for, and why?
4. Would a separate utility reduce measurable staff time/error enough to justify adoption?
5. What security and professional-trust requirements must a tool meet?

Do not claim a gap until these questions are answered with direct evidence.

## New audit finding — 23 October 2026

The free Filingo and Charter AI tools materially weaken Candidate D's generic MVP. Before any build, test these live tools and compare their input fields, outputs, evidence trail, current-form support, security model and edge-case handling. Public vendor pages establish advertised features only; they do not establish reliability or customer adoption. However, the overlap is strong enough to rule out building a basic calculator as currently proposed.
