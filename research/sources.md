## Source quality labels
- **Official/primary:** government publications, official platform/product documentation.
- **Vendor-published:** feature and price descriptions by the company selling the product.
- **Independent reporting:** credible reporting not controlled by the vendor.
- **Customer evidence:** interviews, workflow observations, paid pilots, retention data.
- **Assumption:** not verified yet.

Never treat vendor dashboard numbers, testimonials or marketing outcomes as independently audited unless there is independent evidence.
