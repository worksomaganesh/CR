# Sales and customer acquisition

CR's intended offer inventory and outcome-based package hypotheses live in the [service catalogue](../docs/service-catalogue.md). It covers AI marketing, knowledge infrastructure, sales systems, customer operations, websites/integrations, analytics and custom technical solutions. Treat them as a broad menu of capabilities, not validated demand or a promise to sell everything.

## Sales philosophy
We are not trying to pressure people into buying an AI product. We are trying to find businesses with a painful, recurring problem and show a measurable improvement.

## Early funnel
1. Research a narrow ICP.
2. Verify business and relevance evidence.
3. Reach out through a legitimate published business channel.
4. Ask for a short discovery conversation.
5. Understand the current workflow and alternatives.
6. Offer a tailored demo only when relevant.
7. Propose a tightly scoped pilot with clear success criteria.
8. Record objections, outcomes and next steps.
9. Request permission before using a customer story publicly.

## Offer packaging and sales boundaries

- At any one time, focus on one or two offers with a clear buyer, recurring workflow, measurable outcome and plausible paid-pilot path.
- Sell outcomes rather than technical labels such as RAG, agents or Power BI. Explain the method only when it helps the buyer decide.
- Define deliverables, quantities/volumes, revision limits, supported channels, integrations, customer responsibilities, exclusions, acceptance criteria and support boundaries in writing.
- Marketing content does not guarantee leads or sales; competitor reports must cite dated public sources and distinguish observation from inference.
- Proposal/quote generators use customer-approved facts and pricing rules. Human approval is required for final prices, discounts, contractual commitments and consequential customer-facing actions unless a validated authorised process explicitly permits automation.
- For dashboards/analytics, distinguish a static Excel/Sheets report, a Power BI `.pbix` handover, published/sharable Power BI, and managed automated reporting. Confirm licensing, access and refresh requirements before quoting.
- Separate one-time implementation from recurring support, platform subscriptions, messaging/API usage and other third-party charges.

## Early metrics
Track researched leads, verified leads, messages sent, replies, discovery meetings, qualified pain signals, demos, pilot proposals, paid pilots, collected revenue and renewals. Keep denominators clear; do not cherry-pick conversion rates.

## Voice AI
Do not use bulk automated cold calling as the initial acquisition method. Before any calling campaign, verify current TRAI/DLT/TSP rules, consent/permission requirements, caller registration/number requirements and provider terms. Use a vendor only after the use case and compliance path are understood.
