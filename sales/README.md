# Sales and customer acquisition

## Sales philosophy
We are not trying to pressure people into buying an AI product. We are trying to find businesses with a painful, recurring problem and show a measurable improvement.

## Early funnel
1. Research a narrow ICP.
2. Verify business and relevance evidence.
3. Reach out through a legitimate published business channel.
4. Ask for a short discovery conversation.
5. Understand the current workflow and alternatives.
6. Offer a tailored demo only when relevant.
7. Propose a tightly scoped pilot with clear success criteria.
8. Record objections, outcomes and next steps.
9. Request permission before using a customer story publicly.

## Early metrics
Track researched leads, verified leads, messages sent, replies, discovery meetings, qualified pain signals, demos, pilot proposals, paid pilots, collected revenue and renewals. Keep denominators clear; do not cherry-pick conversion rates.

## Voice AI
Do not use bulk automated cold calling as the initial acquisition method. Before any calling campaign, verify current TRAI/DLT/TSP rules, consent/permission requirements, caller registration/number requirements and provider terms. Use a vendor only after the use case and compliance path are understood.
