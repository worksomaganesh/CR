# CA Discovery Outreach — Research Only

**Purpose:** Request a brief conversation about the current workflow, not sell a product.  
**Current product status:** No validated product, no prototype, no client interviews yet.  
**Audience:** CA firms that publicly advertise audit, tax and compliance services.  
**Do not send automatically.** Personalise, verify the published business contact, and send only after human review.

## Suggested first message

Subject: Student research on MSME-payment working papers and Form No. 26

Hello,

I’m a first-year B.Tech AIML student researching how CA firms organise the MSME-payment compliance working papers and how they are adapting existing workflows to Form No. 26 for Tax Year 2026–27.

I’m not selling software or providing tax advice; I’m trying to understand how firms currently do the work and what tools they already use. Would someone on your audit/tax team be open to a brief 10–12 minute research conversation? I’m particularly interested in the steps that are already working well, as well as any manual rework or gaps you have actually encountered.

No client documents or confidential information are needed. If a conversation is inconvenient, a short pointer to a public resource or the right role to learn from would also be helpful.

Thank you for considering it.

Regards,  
A student researcher

## Follow-up policy
- Send one polite follow-up after 5–7 business days only if appropriate.
- If no response, stop; do not repeatedly message or switch to personal channels.
- Honour any request not to contact again.
- Do not use automated cold calls or bulk WhatsApp outreach.

## First call structure (10–12 minutes)
1. Explain the study and ask permission to take notes.
2. Ask the respondent to describe their most recent actual workflow end-to-end.
3. Ask which accounting system, tax/audit software, spreadsheets and source records they used.
4. Ask which steps were automatic vs manual; ask for one recent example of rework, missing information or correction.
5. Ask how the team is adapting to Form No. 26 for Tax Year 2026–27.
6. Ask what the current tools already handle well and why the firm selected them.
7. Ask what would make a new utility untrusted or unnecessary.
8. Only if a real gap emerges, ask whether they would review a synthetic example later.

## Safety and research rules
- Do not request client names, PANs, Udyam certificates, Tally backups, ledgers, invoices or other confidential client records during first discovery.
- Do not record a call unless explicit permission is granted.
- Keep notes anonymised and summarise the workflow rather than storing unnecessary identifying details.
- Do not claim a statutory interpretation is correct; invite professional correction.
- Do not describe a product as “audit-ready” or promise compliance.
- The goal is to learn, including discovering that no product is needed.
