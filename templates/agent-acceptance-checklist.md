# Agent Output Acceptance Checklist

Use this to review research, plans, code, marketing drafts and operations artifacts before marking them Accepted.

## General checks

- [ ] The task matched the original brief and stayed within scope.
- [ ] The correct current CR files and decisions were used.
- [ ] Facts, vendor claims, assumptions, interpretations and unknowns are distinguished.
- [ ] Important external claims have traceable sources and dates.
- [ ] No fabricated sources, customers, metrics, tests, results or contact details appear.
- [ ] The output states what was not done or could not be verified.
- [ ] Data permissions and confidentiality rules were followed.
- [ ] Risks, dependencies and escalation needs are visible.
- [ ] The artifact is saved in the correct repository/folder.
- [ ] A human or independent reviewer accepts the result.

## Research-specific checks

- [ ] Primary sources were preferred and links were checked.
- [ ] Evidence against the hypothesis was actively considered.
- [ ] Competitors and manual substitutes were included.
- [ ] Desk research is not described as customer validation.
- [ ] Willingness to pay is not inferred from compliments or market size.
- [ ] Recommended next step is a falsifiable test.

## Code-specific checks

- [ ] The change matches the approved spec.
- [ ] Tests/checks were actually run in the stated environment.
- [ ] Security, privacy, permissions and data handling were reviewed.
- [ ] No secrets, customer files or unrelated changes were committed.
- [ ] Staging/preview evidence is included where appropriate.
- [ ] Production deployment and rollback have human approval.
- [ ] Implementer did not self-approve the change.

## External-content checks

- [ ] The intended audience and purpose are clear.
- [ ] Every factual claim has evidence.
- [ ] Project status and personal contribution are accurate.
- [ ] Customer names, testimonials, logos and screenshots have permission.
- [ ] Asset rights and platform requirements are respected.
- [ ] Exact final copy and destination have founder approval.
- [ ] The live URL is recorded only after publication is verified.
